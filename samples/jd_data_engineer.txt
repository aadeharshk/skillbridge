Data Engineer (Sample JD, fictional company)

What you'll do
- Design, build and maintain batch ETL pipelines that move data from operational databases into our cloud data warehouse.
- Write efficient SQL and Python to transform raw data into clean, well-modelled tables for analytics.
- Orchestrate pipelines with Apache Airflow and monitor data quality and freshness.
- Work with analysts and business stakeholders to understand reporting needs.

Requirements
- 1-3 years of experience in data engineering or data analytics.
- Strong SQL and Python skills.
- Experience with PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Hands-on experience with a cloud platform, preferably AWS (S3, Glue, Redshift).
- Understanding of data modelling (star schema) and data warehousing concepts.
- Familiarity with Git and version control.

Nice to have
- Experience with Apache Spark or PySpark.
- Exposure to dbt or Snowflake.
- Experience with Docker.
